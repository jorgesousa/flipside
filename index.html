How a flip works

Take a game everyone already knows. Keep every rule. Hand the player the other side.

Same rules

Same board, same walls, same speed-up. Nothing new to learn before the fun starts.

Other side

You control the thing that was built to be eaten, smashed or shot.

The hero turns boss

The famous character is now the computer. It plays to win, and it doesn't feel sorry for you.

New ways to win

Survive longer, steal the bonus, or make the hero fail at its own game.
